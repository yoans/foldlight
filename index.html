How far can a picture nest?

Foldlight is a playable loop: two virtual monitors mixed through glass, rods for scale and rotation, trap, delay, and a mode where two structures write each other. Built after Dave Blair’s Light Herder and Peter King’s 1997 glass trick. Hofstadter wrote Blair. If Blair wants a different name or the site down, that is his call.
