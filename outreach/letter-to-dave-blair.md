# Letter to Dave Blair — seeking blessing

**To:** Dave Blair / The Light Herder  
**Via:** [thelightherder.com](https://www.thelightherder.com/) (Blogger comment or post), [YouTube: The Light Herder](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=The+Light+Herder+4K+video+feedback), Instagram [DaveBlairLeft](https://www.instagram.com/) / DaveBlairRight, [davidblairportfolio.com](https://davidblairportfolio.com/video-feedback-kinetic-sculpture/), Make: author profile  
**From:** Nathaniel Young, [Build Beyond Belief](https://buildbeyondbelief.com/)  
**Re:** A public software homage to the Device — your call whether it stays up

---

Dave,

I built a browser instrument that tries to be *analogous* to your Light Herder, not a replacement for it. It is live at **https://foldlight.buildbeyondbelief.com**. I am writing to ask for your blessing — and to take it down, rename it, or change the credits if you do not want this in the world under that light.

I watched the 4K walkthrough. I read the King / Sweet and Fizzy 1997 glass note you shout out. I took seriously the thing you keep repeating: the operator is in the loop, the knobs are the middlespace, the glass is why you get fractals and not just tunnels, trap/cut is how an image starts living in the wires, delay slows the dying, insanity is two structures making each other.

What I did **not** do is pretend a shader is optics. There is no air gap, no mahogany, no analog potentiometer on a 4K panel. The pictures are GPU ping-pong: contracted copies screened together the way two monitors mix in 50/50 glass. It is software. People using Hydra or TouchDesigner already make digital feedback. What I wanted in fans’ hands was *your topology* — rods, glass, trap, delay, five-ish analog grades, two structures, a front loop — playable on a phone, with the console still there for people who want to get lost.

I will keep the site honest:

- Your name, your site, Peter King, Hofstadter, Magritte, the 1988 dorm-room origin.
- A clear line: this is homage, unauthorized until you say otherwise, not the Device, not “fractals without a computer.”
- If you want a note from you on the page, I will put it first. If you want the name changed, I will change it. If you want it gone, it goes.

I am not selling the instrument. It is a public proof. I do technical coaching (messy physical/digital builds) and I linked that lightly for people who want that kind of help. The fans get the toy for free.

Thank you for decades of herding light. The 4K version is one of the most specific beautiful machines I have ever seen explained.

Nathaniel Young  
https://buildbeyondbelief.com  
https://foldlight.buildbeyondbelief.com

## How to actually get this in front of him

There is no public email on thelightherder.com. Use several thin, respectful touches rather than one blast:

1. **YouTube comment** on the 4K device video — he reads those; that is how Austin White reached him about analog knobs on 4K panels.
2. **Blogger comment** on a recent Light Herder post (comments are open on the site).
3. **Instagram DM** to DaveBlairLeft / DaveBlairRight (the split-feed pair).
4. **Make: article** comment on *Freaky Fractals* (Feb 2022) — he is the author; editors sometimes relay.
5. **LEAF / gallery people** if you have a warmer intro (Palm Beach Cultural Council, Lafayette Electronic Arts Festival collaborators).
6. Do **not** scrape people-search sites for a home address or phone.

Offer a private preview URL first if the public subdomain is not live yet. Attach a still. Keep the first message under ~150 words; point to this longer letter.
